Vendor details
Datadog Active

Datadog reported Q2 2026 results on Aug 6: revenue $1.12B (+36% YoY), non-GAAP EPS $0.65 (vs. $0.58 consensus), free cash flow $279M. Customer count with $100K+ ARR grew 23% YoY to ~4,720. Q3 guidance: $1.135–1.145B revenue.

At DASH 2026, Datadog announced 100+ capabilities centred on autonomous AI operations: Bits AI for end-to-end incident detection, investigation, and remediation; AI Guard to protect AI agents from prompt injection and data poisoning; Bits Agent Builder for custom AI agent creation; and Bring Your Own Cloud (BYOC) to deploy Datadog within customer-controlled environments. Bits Code and Bits Chat also moved to GA.

ClickHouse Active

On Aug 3, 2026, ClickHouse announced the launch of ClickHouse Labs, a dedicated research organisation, with Andrew "Andy" Pavlo — a Carnegie Mellon University database systems professor and industry thought leader — joining as VP of Database Research. Pavlo is a leading voice in the database community and the hire signals serious investment in foundational query engine and systems research.

This follows ClickHouse's milestone-heavy May 2026: $250M ARR, 4,000 customers, Claude-powered Agents launch, CostBench (open cost-performance benchmark), and the House Mates partner program. The Labs initiative positions ClickHouse as a research-driven database company, not just a fast analytics cloud.

Dynatrace Active

Dynatrace reported Q1 FY2027 results on Aug 5, 2026: revenue $554.55M (+16% YoY, above ~$549.5M consensus), ARR growth 17%, and 41% organic net new ARR growth. Non-GAAP EPS $0.48 beat the $0.41 consensus by 17%. FY2027 EPS guidance raised to $1.97–1.99 (vs. ~$1.78 consensus), driving a 12% stock surge. Free cash flow margin: 56%; balance sheet debt-free.

CFO James Benson announced plans to retire by March 2027. Management highlighted continued rollout of AI-driven Dynatrace Intelligence capabilities and deeper platform integrations, reinforcing the autonomous operations narrative established at Perform 2026.

Previews indicate live demos of New Relic Autopilot, Ground Truth, and Preflight, plus APM logs in context going live on Aug 12. Splunk / Cisco Active

On Aug 4, 2026, Splunk announced that key Cisco Data Fabric platform innovations are generally available: Machine Data Lake, Catalog, Agent Launchpad, and expanded Federated Search and Data Management capabilities. These let teams find, connect, and query machine data across distributed environments without centralising it.

New capabilities include AI-powered data management, natural-language investigation via AI Canvas, and custom AI agent creation via Agent Builder. The GA marks the realisation of Cisco's "Agentic Enterprise" architecture announced at .conf25 — shifting Splunk from a log aggregation platform toward a distributed, AI-native data fabric for security and IT ops.

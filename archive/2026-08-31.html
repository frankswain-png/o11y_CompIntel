Vendor details
Datadog Active

No product or partnership announcement landed in the window — the movement was all in press and market sentiment. DDOG opened 28 August at $234.87, down $2.10, giving back part of the 6.7% rebound to $242.93 reported in the previous pulse. Motley Fool published a "buy or sell?" assessment the same day, and Yahoo Finance ran a piece framing Datadog as a promising cloud stock for 2026. The sell-side picture firmed slightly: 46 analysts now average a strong buy with a $285.18 twelve-month target, roughly 20% above the current level. The unresolved question behind the volatility is still the guided spending slowdown from Datadog's largest AI-native customer, understood to be OpenAI, which drove the ~16% post-Q2 drop on 6 August.

Watch signal: Datadog's story is now visibly dependent on a single AI-native account, which is a concentration argument Elastic can raise in enterprise evaluations — while watching for whether the pressure translates into more aggressive discounting on Q3 renewals.

Grafana Labs Quiet

No significant updates in the past 72 hours. Last tracked: Loki 3.5 shipping cadence (Helm chart improvements, SQLite-backed delete requests, LogQL and storage enhancements) on top of Grafana 13.

ClickHouse Active

The Open House roadshow continued through the window, reaching Boston on 31 August alongside a data trivia night, following the San Francisco stop on 28 August. The remaining legs run Amsterdam (1 September), New York (10 September), Singapore (24 September) and London (30 September) — six cities in six weeks. The content is anchored on the August newsletter: the 26.7 release, ClickHouse Managed Postgres, and updates across observability and vector search, plus a new database research lab and an interactive playground covering 100+ databases.

Watch signal: ClickHouse is pitching one engine for logs, analytics and vector search — Elastic's own consolidation story told at a lower storage cost point — and the roadshow cadence means field teams should expect it named in more evaluations through September.

Vendor details
Datadog Earnings watch

Datadog's Q2 FY2026 earnings call is set for August 6 (before market open). Analysts expect revenue of $1.08B (+26% YoY) and EPS of $0.58. The stock has surged roughly 80% year-to-date on strong AI infrastructure and observability demand. CEO Olivier Pomel has been selling shares.

The last major product push was at DASH 2026 (June) where Datadog shipped 100+ features including expanded Bits AI agents, AI Guard for agentic security, Bring Your Own Cloud for logs, and Agent Console — a centralised hub for monitoring AI coding tools including Claude Code and Cursor.

Grafana Labs Major release

Grafana concluded AI Week (July 27–31) with six GA agentic operations capabilities: Grafana Assistant Investigations (autonomous hypothesis formation and data swarming), Grafana Assistant Workspace, Grafana Assistant Automations, the Grafana Cloud MCP server, gcx (a CLI for agentic workflows), and Grafana Agent Observability. Post-event press and community coverage landed this week.

Grafana was also named a Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader for Observability Platforms in July, positioned furthest in Completeness of Vision — a significant analyst win that compounds AI Week's market impact.

ClickHouse Quiet

No press releases or partnership announcements this window. The mx-central-1 (Mexico Central) AWS region went live for ClickPipes — a routine geo-expansion noted in the cloud changelog. The company's last major news cycle was Open House 2026 in May, when it surpassed $250M ARR and launched Claude-powered ClickHouse Agents.

Dynatrace Earnings day

Dynatrace released Q1 FY2027 financial results this morning (Aug 5, before market open). Consensus projected ~$549M revenue and $0.44 EPS. Full-year FY2027 guidance targets $2.32–2.34B revenue (+14–15% YoY) and 15.5–16.5% ARR growth, targeting ARR of $2.38–2.40B.

On July 15, Dynatrace was named a Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader for Observability Platforms for the 16th consecutive time. Chandu Thota, an AI and technology leader, was appointed to the Board of Directors on July 29, signalling continued AI governance investment.

New Relic Preview launch

New Relic eBPF Logs entered public preview on July 31. The feature delivers kernel-level log collection without deploying instrumentation agents, complementing the eAPM General Availability from December 2025. eBPF gives New Relic a low-friction path into infrastructure accounts previously requiring full agent deployment.

Earlier in 2026 New Relic launched New Relic Preflight for AI Coding Observability and committed to FedRAMP High / DoD IL4 authorizations — expanding its reach into regulated industries where Elastic also competes.

Splunk Quiet

No major corporate announcements in this window. Splunk held regional user group meetings in Twin Cities (Aug 4) and Columbus, OH (Aug 5) focused on AI-assisted dashboard creation. The ongoing Cisco integration continues to consolidate the Splunk Partnerverse into Cisco's 360 Partner Program. Splunk Federated Search for Snowflake went GA in July for Splunk Cloud AWS commercial customers — the most recent notable product milestone.

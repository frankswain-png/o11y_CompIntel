Dynatrace Active

Dynatrace announced (Aug 13) a definitive agreement to acquire Arize, a unified AI observability and LLM-evaluation platform, for $915M — roughly $815M in cash plus replacement equity for Arize employees. The deal, expected to close in Q3 2026, aims to bridge the gap between AI eval/testing tooling and production observability, an integration Dynatrace's own team calls fragmented today.

This lands right after Dynatrace's Q1 FY27 results (Aug 5), which showed 41% organic net-new ARR growth but a trimmed full-year revenue guide and a planned CFO transition.

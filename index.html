Vendor details
DatadogActive

Datadog announced a strategic partnership with public-sector reseller Vertosoft (Aug 18) to bring its FedRAMP High-certified Datadog for Government offering to federal agencies. No new product launches were reported this cycle.

Watch signal: Reinforces Datadog's push into regulated/government workloads — a segment where Elastic's FedRAMP-authorized offerings compete directly.

Grafana LabsActive

Grafana Labs continued iterating on its logging stack, shipping Loki 3.6 with a horizontally scalable compactor and native OpenTelemetry tracing support, alongside ongoing expansion of AI Observability and Grafana Assistant.

Watch signal: Deeper OTel-native tracing in Loki narrows the gap with Elastic's integrated logging and APM story.

ClickHouseActive

ClickHouse announced a strategic partnership with Experience Engineering firm Ciklum to accelerate enterprise adoption of agentic AI and real-time analytics, and shipped LTS point release v25.8.32.4 (Aug 21).

Watch signal: The Ciklum tie-up extends ClickHouse's systems-integrator bench for AI/analytics workloads — track for competitive displacement risk in real-time analytics deals.

DynatraceActive

Dynatrace signed a definitive agreement to acquire AI observability vendor Arize for $915M (Aug 13) and, on Aug 20, privately placed $1.4375B in 0% exchangeable senior notes due 2031 to help fund the deal and strengthen its balance sheet.

Watch signal: Significantly deepens Dynatrace's LLM/agent observability stack — direct competitive pressure on Elastic's AI Observability and agent-monitoring roadmap.

New RelicQuiet

No significant announcements found in the past 72 hours.

SplunkActive

Splunk (Cisco) joined Atlassian in a coordinated disclosure (Aug 20) of fixes for over 250 vulnerabilities across products, including several critical/high-severity RCE and auth-bypass flaws, plus a dedicated Splunk Enterprise security hardening release.

Watch signal: A high-volume vulnerability disclosure keeps patch management and security posture top-of-mind for Splunk customers — an opening for Elastic Security in competitive conversations.

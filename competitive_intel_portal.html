Vendor details
Datadog Active

Shares closed at $225.77 on Aug 24, down 4.2% on the day and well off highs, extending the drop that followed Q2 FY2026 results on Aug 6. Revenue came in at $1.12B (+36% YoY) and beat estimates, but forward guidance implied slower growth and the market has kept selling. Separately, a pricing change was detected across Datadog's SKUs on Aug 21 — one of six tracked product updates this month.

Watch signal: Sustained multiple compression tends to make Datadog more aggressive on price and more insistent on platform bundling, so Elastic reps should expect discount-led defence on renewals and pre-empt with total-cost and data-ownership framing.

Grafana Labs Active

No press-release-level news in the window, but the engineering cadence continues: Loki 3.5 landed with broad Helm chart improvements, SQLite support for delete requests, a revised meta-monitoring guide, and distributor, operator, storage and LogQL enhancements. This sits on top of Grafana 13 (GrafanaCON, April) and the Grafana Assistant's expansion to natural-language querying across 30+ data sources.

Watch signal: Grafana keeps chipping away at the operational gaps that used to make Loki a non-starter at scale, so the "we already run Grafana" objection is getting harder to dislodge in log-management deals.

ClickHouse Active

Two customer-facing events ran inside the window — "PostgreSQL and ClickHouse, better together" (Aug 25) and "Data architecture blueprints featuring Zappi" (Aug 26) — reinforcing the migration-and-interop motion. The August newsletter covers ClickHouse 26.7 with updates to Managed Postgres, observability and vector search. Momentum context: $250M+ ARR, 4,000 customers, Claude-powered ClickHouse Agents, the CostBench open benchmark, and the House Mates partner programme (25+ ISV/tech, 35+ services partners, including a launch partnership with Ciklum announced Aug 18).

Watch signal: CostBench plus a maturing partner channel means ClickHouse is arming third parties to run cost comparisons against Elastic, so a validated response to that benchmark is worth having on hand.

Dynatrace Active

Nothing newly announced in the 72-hour window, but two August moves are still working through the market. On Aug 13 Dynatrace signed a definitive agreement to acquire AI-observability vendor Arize in a cash-and-stock deal valued at $915M, positioning the combined platform to evaluate, operate and improve AI applications from development into production. On Aug 20, Dynatrace LLC privately placed $1.4375B of 0.00% exchangeable senior notes due 2031, netting roughly $1.411B — a capital restructuring that comfortably funds the acquisition.

Watch signal: Dynatrace is buying category leadership in LLM evaluation rather than building it, so Elastic should expect AI-observability requirements to appear in enterprise RFPs sooner than planned.

New Relic Quiet

No significant announcements in the past 72 hours. Most recent activity dates to earlier in August: Notebooks reached GA (Aug 11), Google Cloud observability enhancements went GA (Aug 4), APM logs-in-context opened up (Aug 12), and a GitHub MCP integration linking Change Tracking deployments to pull requests entered public preview.

Splunk / Cisco Quiet

No significant announcements in the past 72 hours. The standing story remains the Aug 4 general availability of Splunk platform innovations supporting Cisco Data Fabric — federated access to machine data in place, with built-in AI agents and reusable agent skills.

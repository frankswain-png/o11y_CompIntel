Datadog Active

Datadog reported Q2 FY2026 financial results on August 6 before U.S. markets opened. Analysts had set a consensus revenue estimate of $1.08B (+30.6% YoY) and EPS of $0.58 (+26.1% YoY), following a strong Q1 where revenue hit $1.006B (+32% YoY). Management had guided Q2 revenues to $1.07–$1.08B with non-GAAP operating income of $225–$235M.

Separately, on August 5 Datadog announced management will present at the Canaccord Genuity 46th Annual Growth Conference (Aug 12), the Citi Global TMT Conference (Sep 8), and the Goldman Sachs Communacopia & Technology Conference (Sep 10), maintaining high investor visibility heading into H2 2026.

Grafana Labs Quiet

No new press releases or product launches in the Aug 3–6 window. This week follows the close of Grafana's AI Week (July 27–31), during which the company shipped six GA agentic operations capabilities: Grafana Assistant Investigations, Grafana Assistant Workspace, Grafana Assistant Automations, the Grafana Cloud MCP server, gcx (a code instrumentation tool), and Grafana Agent Observability.

Grafana was also named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Observability Platforms, positioned furthest on Completeness of Vision. Check grafana.com/blog for any mid-week releases.

ClickHouse Active

On August 3, ClickHouse launched ClickHouse Labs, a new internal research group, and announced that Andy Pavlo — Carnegie Mellon professor and one of the most recognized names in database systems research — will join as VP of Database Research. Pavlo is known for his work on autonomous databases, OLAP transaction processing, and large-scale analytics.

ClickHouse Labs will pursue foundational research in query processing, storage architectures, systems performance, and infrastructure — covering both ClickHouse and PostgreSQL databases. This follows ClickHouse's Open House 2026 milestone of $250M ARR and 4,000 customers, and the earlier launch of Claude-powered ClickHouse Agents for agentic analytics. The Pavlo hire is a significant credibility signal for enterprise and research-oriented buyers.

Dynatrace Active

Dynatrace reported Q1 FY2027 results on August 5. Revenue came in at $554.5M (+16.2% YoY), missing analyst consensus of $566.5M. EPS of $0.48 beat estimates of $0.41 by 17%, supported by a 56% free cash flow margin and zero debt. ARR grew 17% YoY, and net new ARR grew 41% organically.

The company raised FY2027 adjusted EPS guidance to $1.97–$1.99 (vs. consensus ~$1.78) but set revenue guidance at $2.3B — a range analysts read as conservative relative to prior growth rates. CFO James Benson announced he will retire by March 2027. Management also highlighted new AI-driven Dynatrace Intelligence features and deeper platform integrations as part of their autonomous operations strategy.

New Relic Quiet

No significant product announcements or press releases in the Aug 3–6 window. New Relic is building toward its RUNTIME user conference, co-located with RenderATL on August 12–13 in Atlanta. The event features an opening keynote by Kelsey Hightower, with speakers including Microsoft VP Scott Hanselman, and sessions on AI observability, OpenTelemetry for AI, alert quality management, and agent observability.

New Relic is also launching APM logs in context on August 12, surfacing critical logs alongside telemetry data. Watch this space after the RUNTIME event for potential AI observability positioning shifts.

Splunk / Cisco Active

On August 4, Splunk released Enterprise Security 8.6.1 (GA), a maintenance update that primarily adds the ability to download full documentation manuals as PDFs from help.splunk.com. No new security detections or major capability changes were included in this point release.

The larger story is what's coming: .conf26 is scheduled for September 14–17 in Denver, where Cisco and Splunk are expected to showcase their integrated platform story. At Cisco Live 2026 in June, the companies previewed Cisco AI Canvas integration with Splunk Cloud Platform, described as a "virtual war room" for collaborative incident response. Elastic Security should prepare competitive positioning now ahead of September.

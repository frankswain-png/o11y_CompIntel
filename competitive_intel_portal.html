Vendor details
Datadog Active

Datadog's Q2 FY2026 results (reported Aug 6) beat expectations — $1.12B revenue (+36% YoY), EPS $0.65, free cash flow $279M, full-year guidance raised to $4.45–4.47B — but the stock fell 17% on two concerns: management disclosed reduced usage from its largest AI customer, and FCF margins contracted from 29% to 25%. The stock recovered 11.5% on Aug 10 as broader AI-sector sentiment improved.

On Aug 12, Datadog management presented at the Canaccord Genuity 46th Annual Growth Conference. No new product announcements were made in this 72-hour window. The live product narrative remains the DASH 2026 launch (100+ features: Bits AI autonomous ops, AI Guard, Agent Console for coding-agent monitoring).

Grafana Labs Active

Aug 10 — Session Replay in Frontend Observability (public preview): Records user interactions — clicks, scrolls, page state — and correlates them with errors, logs, and traces in a single replay view. Closes a significant gap vs. tools like Datadog RUM and Elastic's own APM frontend agent.

Aug 11 — Adaptive Profiles (GA): Applies a cost-effective baseline sample rate and automatically scales to 100% sampling when behavioural changes are detected, claiming 85–90% reduction in stored profiling volume. This is Grafana's most direct cost-efficiency play for Grafana Cloud Profiles. Grafana was named a Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader in July, furthest on Completeness of Vision axis for the second consecutive year.

ClickHouse Active

Aug 11 — bitdrift joins House Mates integration programme: bitdrift, a mobile-native observability SDK, joined ClickStack's ecosystem, bringing lightweight telemetry capture with minimal overhead to Android, iOS, and React Native applications. The integration feeds mobile traces and debugging data directly into ClickStack.

Broader context: ClickHouse surpassed $250M ARR and 4,000 customers, launched Claude-powered agentic analytics at Open House 2026 in May, and acquired LLM observability startup Langfuse. An APJ VP appointment (Ed Lenta) signals expanding international go-to-market ambition. ClickHouse 26.7 is the current release.

Dynatrace Active

Aug 13 — Acquires Arize for $915M: Dynatrace signed a definitive agreement to acquire Arize, the leading independent AI observability and LLM evaluation platform. The transaction is ~$815M cash plus replacement equity for Arize employees joining Dynatrace. Co-founders Jason Lopatecki and Aparna Dhinakaran join Dynatrace; Lopatecki continues leading the Arize team. Expected to close this quarter or early Q3 Dynatrace FY27, subject to regulatory review.

Arize provides AI tracing, LLM evaluation tooling, and production monitoring for ML and GenAI applications — capabilities directly in Elastic's AI observability build zone. At the KeyBank Technology Leadership Forum on Aug 10, Dynatrace argued observability is a $10B incremental AI market inside an $80B sector growing 40–50% annually, citing Q1 FY27 new logo ARR up 160% YoY.

New Relic Active

Aug 12 — APM logs in context (GA): New Relic made APM logs in context generally available, surfacing critical logs inline alongside APM traces and metrics. Users can opt out at any time. This closes a parity gap vs. Elastic APM, which has offered correlated logs-in-context for some time.

Recent broader moves: strategic Microsoft partnership deepening Azure Marketplace commitments (double-digit YoY bookings growth), AI Coding Observability launch (June), New Relic for Startups (June), and appointment of Hein Hellemons as CRO. New Relic Knowledge — integrating telemetry and knowledge sources for AI-era incident resolution — is an emerging differentiator to watch.

Splunk Quiet

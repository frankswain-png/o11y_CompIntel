ClickHouse Active

The Open House roadshow ran through the window — Boston on 31 August, Amsterdam on 1 September — with New York (10 September), Singapore (24 September) and London (30 September) still to come. The content is anchored on the August newsletter: the 26.7 release, in which text indexes now store token positions and delivered a reported 40× speedup on a phrase query, alongside join optimisations; ClickHouse Managed Postgres; and updates across observability and vector search. Separately, Andy Pavlo has joined to establish ClickHouse Labs, which will work with the Postgres team on performance and reliability, and Ciklum signed on as a featured launch partner in the House Mates programme on 18 August.

Watch signal: Positional text indexing moves ClickHouse from "fast aggregations" to plausible phrase search over logs, which narrows the technical gap in exactly the workload Elastic wins on today — worth a close look from the search relevance side, not just pricing.

Standing items: the Arize acquisition announced 13 August remains pending; the $1.25B exchangeable notes offering disclosed 20 August is partly earmarked for buybacks. The State of SRE and Platform Engineering 2026 study published 25 August is the freshest thought-leadership asset — 919 senior IT leaders, with monitoring AI systems now the top SRE use case at 58% and AI-powered features ranked the most important platform capability by 67%.

New Relic Active

New Relic's Japan region went generally available on 31 August, closing out a plan first announced in March. The region is reached at one.jp.newrelic.com, is available exclusively through New Relic's Japan entity, and documentation has been refreshed across the product set to reflect region-specific capabilities and endpoints. It follows a steady August cadence: Notebooks GA on 11 August, Google Cloud observability enhancements GA on 4 August, the New Relic for Startups programme, and Hein Hellemons joining as Chief Revenue Officer.

Watch signal: This is a go-to-market move as much as an infrastructure one — a local entity plus in-country data plane removes the residency objection that has slowed New Relic in regulated Japanese accounts, so Elastic should expect tougher APJ competition and be ready to counter on region coverage, sovereign options and total cost.

Splunk / Cisco Quiet

No significant updates in the past 72 hours. Last tracked: Observability Cloud release notes dated 19 and 27 August, including an entity-centric Metrics Explorer for faster data discovery and an enhanced ServiceNow integration. The broader positioning remains "agentic observability" — Splunk Agent Observability powered by Cisco's Galileo acquisition, plus AI infrastructure monitoring with Cisco AI POD support — sitting on the 4 August Cisco Data Fabric general availability.

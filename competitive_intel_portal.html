Vendor details
Datadog Active

Datadog reported Q2 FY2026 results on August 6, delivering $1.12B in revenue — 36% YoY growth — well ahead of the $1.08B consensus estimate. Non-GAAP EPS of $0.65 beat estimates of $0.58. The company now has 4,720 customers with $100K+ ARR (up 23% YoY), and 58% of customers use four or more products.

Alongside earnings, Datadog announced GA of its autonomous AI operations suite: Bits AI for end-to-end incident detection, investigation, and remediation; AI Guard for protecting AI agents from prompt injection and poisoning; Bring Your Own Cloud for deploying Datadog within customer environments; and Bits Agent Builder for creating custom AI agents.

Q3 guidance: $1.135–1.145B revenue; full-year 2026 guidance raised to $4.45–4.47B (+30% YoY).

Grafana Labs Active

Grafana Labs closed out its AI Week (July 27–31) with an agentic operations layer going GA on July 28 — six AI-powered capabilities extending Grafana Assistant into end-to-end incident management including detection, investigation, and remediation. The week's community recap published August 3 confirmed strong adoption signal.

Grafana Assistant now covers 30+ data sources with natural-language querying, incident correlation, and query generation across distributed systems. The GrafanaCON 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant placement (furthest Completeness of Vision among Leaders) gives the narrative additional analyst credibility heading into fall procurement cycles.

Grafana 13.1 has also shipped with observability-as-code updates and further extensions to the Assistant feature set for self-managed OSS and Enterprise deployments.

ClickHouse Active

On August 3, ClickHouse announced the launch of ClickHouse Labs, a new internal research group, with Andy Pavlo — award-winning professor at Carnegie Mellon University's Computer Science Department and longtime leader of the CMU Database Research Group — joining as VP of Database Research.

The lab will pursue foundational research in query processing, database architecture, systems performance, and infrastructure for both ClickHouse and PostgreSQL — with advances aimed at real-time analytics, data warehousing, observability, and AI applications. ClickHouse also released version 26.7.3.19 on August 6 (patch release), with 26.8 LTS slated as the next major release.

ClickHouse previously topped $250M ARR and 4,000 customers at its Open House 2026 event in May, where it also launched Claude-powered agentic capabilities.

Dynatrace Active

Dynatrace reported Q1 FY2027 results on August 5, beating the high end of all guidance metrics. Total ARR reached $2.14B (+17% YoY); the company recorded 41% organic net new ARR growth — a record quarter. Revenue came in at $555M (+15% YoY), with subscription revenue at $530M.

The company also announced that long-serving CFO Jim Benson will retire by March 31, 2027. Dynatrace has initiated an external CFO search. CEO Rick McConnell described it as a planned transition with continuity assured through fiscal year-end.

On the product side, Dynatrace open-sourced dtmgd, a kubectl-style CLI for Dynatrace Managed deployments — a nod to developer experience. The community newsletter for August highlights ongoing investment in AI-driven autonomous operations and deeper Kubernetes integrations.

New Relic Active

New Relic's primary near-term news hook is its inaugural RUNTIME user conference, co-located with RenderATL in Atlanta on August 12–13. The event targets developers and SREs with sessions on AI observability, hands-on AWS OpenTelemetry workshops, and keynotes from Kelsey Hightower, Grammy-winning artist and activist Killer Mike, and Microsoft VP Scott Hanselman.

The event signals New Relic's strategy to deepen developer-community presence at a high-profile Black tech conference — a differentiated reach play compared to traditional enterprise observability events. New Relic's recently announced Agentic Platform (Feb 2026) and Autopilot features (June 2026) form the product backdrop for the conference's AI observability messaging.

No major product launches were identified within the strict Aug 7–10 window; the RUNTIME conference itself (Aug 12) is the next significant momentum event to watch.

Splunk Active

Splunk shipped Enterprise Security 8.6.1 to GA on August 4, with incremental enhancements including full manual PDF downloads for offline compliance review. The release continues Cisco's steady post-acquisition cadence of enterprise security hardening.

Separately, Splunk was named a Leader in the IDC MarketScape: Worldwide SIEM 2026 Vendor Assessment, reinforcing its position as Cisco's premier security observability platform. Cisco has been embedding agentic AI across Splunk's security and observability products throughout 2026 — six specialized AI agents in Enterprise Security were announced at RSAC 2026, each targeting specific SOC workflows.

The Splunk Observability Cloud also continues to expand its AI Troubleshooting Agent capabilities, correlating metrics, events, logs, and traces with automated root-cause summaries — a capability set that competes directly with Elastic's observability AI story.

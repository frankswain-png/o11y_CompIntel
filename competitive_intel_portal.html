Vendor details
Datadog Active

DDOG closed 27 August up 6.7% at $242.93, trading between $234.02 and $251.99 — a full reversal of the 24 August decline reported in yesterday's pulse. Commentary attributes the move to dip-buying after post-earnings profit-taking, with enterprise cloud-monitoring and AI-workload demand cited as the fundamental support. Average analyst price target sits at $284.12, though GF Value pegs fair value at $203.40, so the stock is now trading roughly 19% above that estimate. Bloomberg separately covered the ~$3B windfall accruing to Datadog's French founders, keeping the company visible in general business media.

Watch signal: With the multiple restored, the aggressive-discounting scenario looks less likely near term — Elastic should expect Datadog to lead on AI observability and platform bundling in competitive deals rather than price.

Grafana Labs Active

No press-release-level announcement landed in the window. The shipping cadence continues: Loki 3.5 brought broad Helm chart improvements, SQLite support for delete requests, a revised meta-monitoring guide, and distributor, operator, storage and LogQL enhancements. This builds on Grafana 13 from GrafanaCON in April and the Grafana Assistant's expansion to natural-language querying across 30+ data sources, plus the five-year AWS strategic collaboration agreement signed in March.

Watch signal: Each Loki release erodes the operational-maturity argument Elastic has relied on in mid-market log-management deals, so total-cost and retention-at-scale framing needs to carry more of the weight.

ClickHouse Active

The Open House roadshow ran Melbourne on 26 August and reaches San Francisco today, 28 August, with Boston on 31 August — six cities over six weeks. The content leans on the August newsletter and the 26.7 release: Managed Postgres, observability, vector search, text indexes storing token positions for phrase search (a Hacker News benchmark query ran 40× faster), and performance work speeding wide-integer comparisons up to 7× and Delta decompression up to 5×. ClickHouse also confirmed Andy Pavlo has joined to establish ClickHouse Labs, a research-and-credibility play aimed squarely at the database community.

Watch signal: The phrase-search index work is the item to track — it moves ClickHouse closer to a credible full-text story and directly into territory Elastic has treated as defensible.
